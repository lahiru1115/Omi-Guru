# Omi rules

Omi is a card game that many people play in Sri Lanka. These are the full rules that Omi Guru uses.

## Players and cards

- 4 players in 2 teams. Partners sit opposite each other.
- 32 cards: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 7 in each suit.
- 4 suits: [S] Spades, [H] Hearts, [D] Diamonds, [C] Clubs.
- Rank order within a suit, high to low: **A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7**.
- Play goes **anticlockwise**. On the table you are at the bottom, your partner at the top, and the opponents on the right and left.

## Deal and trumps

1. The first dealer is chosen at random.
2. The dealer shuffles and deals a **block of 4 cards** to each player, starting with the player to the dealer's right.
3. **The player to the dealer's right** looks at their 4 cards and chooses trumps. Nobody may help.
4. The dealer then deals a second block of 4 cards to each player. Everyone now has 8 cards.

## Playing tricks

- The trump chooser leads the first trick and may play any card.
- The others play in turn, anticlockwise. If you have the suit that was led, **you must play it** (follow suit).
- A player without that suit may play **any** card: a trump or a card of another suit. You don't have to trump.
- **Trick winner:** the highest trump played. If no trump was played, the highest card of the suit led.
- The trick winner leads the next trick.
- All 8 tricks of a round are always played.

## Scoring

The team that wins 5 or more tricks wins the round.

| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| The trump-choosing team wins (5–7 tricks) | {trumpWin}                                           |
| The other team wins (5–7 tricks)          | {defWin}                                             |
| A team wins all 8 tricks (Kapothi, කැපෝති) | {kapothi}                                            |
| A 4–4 tie                                 | 0, and the next round's winner gets {tieBonus} extra |

- Two ties in a row still give only one bonus (this can be changed in settings).
- The first team to reach **{target} points** wins the game. The game ends as soon as a team reaches {target} after a round is scored.
- After every round, the next dealer is the player to the right of the last dealer.

## Illegal moves

The app never lets you play an illegal card.

- In Practice, cards you can't play are dimmed.
- In a Real game, cards are not dimmed. If you tap an illegal card, it shakes and the app tells you which suit you must play.
